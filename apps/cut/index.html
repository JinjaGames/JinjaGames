Start the helper on your computer

A browser cannot fetch videos from other sites by itself, so a small free helper runs on your own computer and does it. Nothing is installed on a JinjaGames server and nothing is paid.

  1. Download the helper and open it. Windows needs nothing else.
  2. If Windows shows a blue warning, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Pick 1 for this computer or 2 for your phone. This page opens by itself, already connected. For the phone, scan the QR code it shows.
Download for Windows (.exe) Mac / Linux (.zip)

Needs Python 3.8 or newer. The Start file offers to install it on Windows. The first start downloads yt-dlp and ffmpeg, which takes a minute.

I have a connection link

    How it works

    1. The helper runs on your computer and listens only to this page, with a secret key that never leaves your devices.
    2. You paste a link here. Your computer downloads the video with yt-dlp and converts it with ffmpeg.
    3. The finished file comes to this browser. On a phone it travels through a free, temporary Cloudflare tunnel that only your link can open.

    Saving to the gallery

    • iPhone: tap Save to Photos, then choose Save Video in the share sheet. MP3 files go to Save to Files.
    • Android: tap Download. The file lands in Downloads and shows up in the gallery.
    • Videos are saved as H.264 + AAC when the source offers it, so phones can play them. Above 1080p many sites only offer other codecs; those play in VLC and most players but the iPhone gallery may refuse them.

    Use it fairly

    Only download what you are allowed to: your own videos, freely licensed work, or content whose owner lets you. Site terms and copyright are your responsibility.
